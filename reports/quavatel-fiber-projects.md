## 1. Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) deployment support

Supported FTTH deployment activities along the service path from the access network toward customer premises. The work included connection checks, service verification, fault reporting, and connectivity troubleshooting to support stable service delivery.

**Project flow:** Access network → connection checks → service verification → customer premises, with fault reporting and troubleshooting support throughout.

## 2. Fiber fault restoration and splicing

Gained practical exposure to fiber fault restoration, fiber splicing, network troubleshooting, and restoration activities used to maintain reliable telecommunications connectivity. This work strengthened practical understanding of physical-link faults and the operational process used to restore service.

**Project flow:** Network fault → investigation → troubleshooting → restoration → splicing → connectivity restored.

## 3. Fiber installation, commissioning, and quality assurance

Participated in field surveys, fiber installation, commissioning, and quality-assurance processes. Supported organized cable routing, infrastructure checks, and documentation of field findings and maintenance activities.

**Project flow:** Field survey → installation → cable routing → infrastructure checks → commissioning → quality assurance → documentation.
